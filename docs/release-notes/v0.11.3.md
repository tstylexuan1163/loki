## What's new / 本次更新

**Sidebar folds beside terminals / 终端页侧栏自动收窄**
- On the terminal page the sidebar becomes a narrow icon rail; open it again with the button at its bottom or
  ⌘B (Windows: Ctrl+Shift+B). Each page remembers your choice.
- 进入终端页时侧栏收成图标栏，点底部按钮或按 ⌘B（Windows：Ctrl+Shift+B）随时展开；终端页和其他页面各自记住你的选择。

**Interface colours / 界面配色**
- **设置 → 外观 → 界面配色** offers loki (unchanged), a warm neutral grey, or colours derived from the terminal
  scheme: pick Dracula or Solarized and the sidebar, buttons and borders follow, day or night included.
- 「设置 → 外观 → 界面配色」可选 loki（原样）、暖灰，或跟随终端配色：换成 Dracula、Solarized 时，侧栏、按钮、
  边框一起换色，日间/夜间也随配色切换。

The interface is in Simplified Chinese. / 界面为简体中文。
