## What's new / 本次更新

**Host categories / 主机分类**
- Put hosts into categories such as 服务器, 交换机 or your own; a host can be in several. Your existing tags
  become categories automatically.
- The hosts page is split into foldable sections per category, with uncategorised hosts last.
  **管理分类** renames, recolours, reorders, deletes and adds categories.
- Open sessions in the sidebar are grouped by category, and quick connect can filter saved hosts by category.
- 主机可以分到「服务器」「交换机」或自定义分类，一台主机可以属于多个分类；原有的标签会自动变成分类。
- 主机页按分类分成可折叠的几块，未分类的放在最后；「管理分类」可以改名、改颜色、调整顺序、删除和新建。
- 侧栏的会话按分类分组，快速连接可以按分类筛选已保存的主机。

**Assistant / AI 助手**
- The "执行了 N 步" list no longer opens and closes by itself while the assistant works. It stays as you left
  it; the header shows the step that is running, and steps waiting for your approval are always visible.
- AI 执行步骤时，「执行了 N 步」不再自己反复展开收起；标题行显示当前步骤，需要确认的步骤始终显示。

**Also / 其他**
- Hosts without a username no longer show a stray `@` in their address.
- 没有用户名的主机，地址前不再显示多余的 `@`。

The interface is in Simplified Chinese. / 界面为简体中文。
